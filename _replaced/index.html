Watch in your language

Our videos are translated into up to 28 languages, with dubbed audio in about 10 of them. To do that, we use a small in-house Localization Tool, built on the YouTube API Services. It adds translated titles, descriptions and subtitles to our own two channels. It is not offered to the public. See the privacy policy for exactly what it accesses.
