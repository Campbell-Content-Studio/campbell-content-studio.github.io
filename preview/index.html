04 — Watch in your languageOur own channels only

Watch in your language.

Our videos have translated titles and descriptions in 28 languages, and dubbed audio in 10 of them. To do that, we use a small in-house Localization Tool, built on the YouTube API Services. It adds translated titles, descriptions and subtitles to our own two channels. It is not offered to the public. See the privacy policy for exactly what it accesses.
